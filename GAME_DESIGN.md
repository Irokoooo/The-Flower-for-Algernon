# ALGERNON — experience baseline

The archived source is [PRD v1.0](docs/ALGERNON_PRD_v1.0.md). This document records accepted presentation refinements; the PRD retains detailed narrative requirements. User decisions override earlier proposals.

- First-person Charlie; no recurring Charlie avatar or portrait.
- Literary illustration with restrained gouache texture, muted sage, warm ochre and ink. Illustrated NPCs breathe, blink, look toward the player and react to interaction.
- Stable objective scenes; cognition changes interpretation and available connections. Decline remembers former understanding.
- English character voices; English plus Simplified Chinese perceived subtitles. Distinct casting proposals, never invented canonical accents.
- Algernon is a natural white mouse in a persistent 3D maze box, default top view with drag orbit and reset. Movement carries meaning; no numeric cognition bar.
- Peak must feel desirable. A later loss must recall an ability actually experienced by the player.
- The first playable build demonstrates opening observation, test, maze, report, transition and bakery interpretation. Later phases stay visible in the roadmap; placeholders are not completed game content.

## Production gate
Prototype procedural artwork and browser speech previews are explicitly provisional. Final illustrated character sheets and recorded English voice require actual assets, stable licensing and human review before being marked ready.
