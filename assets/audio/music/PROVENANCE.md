# Still Room

Original twenty-second composition for ALGERNON: four understated suspended/added-note
harmonies with sparse piano-like tones and soft reflections. Composed and synthesized
locally by `compose.mjs`. No borrowed melodies, samples, models, or external recordings.
LicenseRef-ALGERNON-Original-Prototype: supplied for use, modification and distribution
within this project, with no claim of a third-party stock license. Attribution:
“Still Room — original ALGERNON composition”. Final human listening review is pending.

Run `node assets/audio/music/compose.mjs` to reproduce the mono 22050 Hz PCM WAV.
Register `manifest-proposal.json`; play with `AudioManager.playSound(id, true)`.
